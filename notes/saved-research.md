# Saved research (not on the page)

Researched on 2026-11-09 and kept here in case they're added later. Neither item is on the page.

## Fennel seed

**Why it's not on the page:** for bacteria, parasites, viruses, fungi, and biofilms, every result found was lab-only. No study where fennel was swallowed was found for those targets. Its main compound (estragole) also limits it to short-term use.

**Safety and limits**
- Estragole harmed mice at high doses (liver tumors) and can damage DNA. EMA guidance value (2021): 0.05 mg estragole per person per day for adults; use 14 days at most; not recommended in pregnancy or breastfeeding above that value. https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/other/second-draft-revision-1-public-statement-use-herbal-medicinal-products-containing-estragole_en.pdf
- Fennel fruit holds roughly 1 to 2.4 mg estragole per gram (estimate from EMA figures), so ½ tsp (about 1 g) is about 20 to 50 times the guidance value. https://www.ema.europa.eu/documents/herbal-report/assessment-report-foeniculum-vulgare-miller_en.pdf
- Less strict views: van den Berg 2014 margin-of-exposure study judged short-term (1 to 2 weeks) adult use low priority. https://air.unimi.it/retrieve/handle/2434/231873/303322/1-s2.0-S0278691513008570-main.pdf ; FEMA panel (Smith 2002) found no significant cancer risk at dietary levels (industry panel). https://repository.tno.nl/SingleDoc?docId=9889
- Older EMA fennel monograph: up to 2 g powder a day, 2 weeks at most. Not for children under 4. Skip with carrot-family allergy (celery, dill, anise, coriander, caraway).

**Studies**
- Digestion, Human: curcumin plus fennel oil for IBS, 121 adults, 30 days: symptoms down 50% vs 26% (Portincasa 2016; two authors from a drug company). https://www.jgld.ro/jgld/index.php/jgld/article/view/1100
- Digestion, Human: fennel seed oil for baby colic, 125 infants: colic gone in 65% vs 24% (Alexandrovich 2003). https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12868253/
- Digestion, Human: 4-seed mix including 4 g fennel for indigestion, 67 adults, 2 weeks (Samimi 2024; mixed product, stopped early). https://brieflands.com/articles/jjnpp-148163
- Digestion, Animal: fennel extract by mouth cut alcohol stomach damage in rats by 68% at 300 mg/kg; lower doses not significant (Birdane 2007). https://www.wjgnet.com/1007-9327/full/v13/i4/607.htm
- Lab only: bacteria (J Med Food 2010, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20136455/), Giardia (Domínguez-Vigil 2022, https://mdpi.com/2223-7747/11/17/2212), Trichomonas (Karami 2019, https://ijpa.tums.ac.ir/index.php/ijpa/article/view/2396), bird flu H5N1 (Dorra 2019, https://journals.ekb.eg/article_29464.html), Candida (Brazilian J Biology 2019, https://scielo.br/j/bjb/a/nphvn3NQHZYpxcGW5Qy9kyv/?lang=en), biofilm 76 to 84% (Nagy-Radványi 2024, https://www.mdpi.com/2076-2607/12/11/2309/htm).

## Pomegranate peel (home-dried, ground)

**Why it's not on the page:** only two swallowed animal studies, no trustworthy human germ study, and no clear tapeworm result.

**Safety**
- Use only the fruit skin, never stems, bark, or root (pelletierine alkaloids). Hong Kong lists the peel as a poisonous part; traditional Chinese medicine uses 3 to 9 g per dose. https://www3.ha.org.hk/toxicplant/en/punica_granatum.html
- Rats given a pomegranate extract (30% punicalagins) for 90 days: no harm at 600 mg/kg a day, the highest dose tested. https://read.qxmd.com/read/18571823/safety-assessment-of-pomegranate-fruit-extract-acute-and-subchronic-toxicity-studies
- Tannins can upset the stomach, add to constipation, and reduce iron absorption. Case reports with warfarin.

**Studies**
- Bacteria, Animal: peel extract by mouth reduced H. pylori stomach inflammation in rats (Mayyas 2021; no numbers in the summary). https://doaj.org/article/852de48520174f61815a6581abf1e151
- Bacteria, Animal: peel extract reduced gut damage and bacteria leaking from the gut in mice with colitis. https://www.aminer.org/pub/5dfa00d2df1a9c0c416494c3/pomegranate-peel-extract-reduced-colonic-damage-and-bacterial-translocation-in-a-mouse
- Viruses, Lab: peel extract blocked flu virus in cells. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC7103433/
- Biofilm, Lab: pomegranate extract acted against bacterial and fungal biofilms. https://www.peeref.com/works/7439855
- Other benefits, Human: 8-week peel extract capsules in type 2 diabetes improved blood pressure and cholesterol (Grabež 2020). https://sova.unibl.org/naucni_rad/beneficial-effects-of-pomegranate-peel-extract-on-plasma-lipid-profile-fatty-acids-levels-and-blood-pressure-in-patients-with-diabetes-mellitus-type-2-a-randomized-double-blind-placebo-cont/
- Left out: tapeworm cysts in mice (Nassef 2024; route unclear, best result was peel plus albendazole). Giardia human trial (al-Muawi 2002; unreliable report).
